# Round 3B — homepage plan (short)

Option 1 (Modern Commerce) stays read-only and is the benchmark. Options 2–4
cover the same business sections as Option 1, each with its own structure.
All four use the same shared sample data (29 lots, 5 sellers, 8 categories,
the live event and 2 upcoming events).

## Option 2 — Visual Marketplace (revised; the visual concept is kept)

- **Header:** unchanged. One bar with a centred logo; see-through over the
  hero; Categories, Search and Menu are full-screen visual overlays.
- **Hero:** unchanged. A centred discovery hero with search inside, framed by
  product cut-outs. Four featured tiles overlap its lower edge.
- **Section order:**
  1. Hero with search
  2. Four featured tiles
  3. Categories photo mosaic (sellers move to their own section)
  4. Live auction: an immersive full-bleed photo block with glass cards and
     the coming-up events
  5. Closing soon: a wide visual rail, with a "Most bid" toggle
  6. Buy Now deals mosaic around a biggest-saving feature tile
  7. Sellers: large photo tiles and the headline stats
  8. Bulk & pallets: mirrored feature rows with manifests
  9. Shop with confidence: a photo-backed band with the 4 trust points
  10. Condition grades band, then how it works
  11. Footer with newsletter
- **Mobile:** see-through top bar; full-screen hero with search; category
  stories; a single-column image feed and swipe rails; an immersive live
  card; no bottom tab bar; a full-screen menu; the live mini-player shrinks
  to a round button.

## Option 3 — Premium Marketplace (new; Marketplace Hub removed)

- **Header:**
  - One solid, refined bar: logo at the start; Shop, Buy Now, Auctions,
    Live and Sellers in the centre; search, watchlist, account, bag and
    language at the end.
  - Shop opens a category panel with photography.
  - Search expands across the bar, with suggestions.
- **Hero:** a split commercial hero. On one side: a large serif headline,
  CTAs and quick category links. On the other: large lifestyle photography
  with a floating featured-lot card.
- **Section order:**
  1. Split hero
  2. Curated categories: an asymmetric editorial grid of all 8
  3. The Buy Now edit: tall portrait product cards with Best value, New
     arrivals and All tabs
  4. Auctions: a large featured lot plus 4 related lots, with a Closing soon
     / Most bid toggle
  5. Featured sellers: storefront previews (cover, monogram, 3 inventory
     thumbnails) and elegant stats
  6. Live salon: the stream, the current lot and bid, and an upcoming
     schedule list
  7. For trade buyers: pallets with manifests, and bulk lots
  8. Buying on Khazna: three editorial columns (confidence, how it works
     with the deposit, the grade scale)
  9. Newsletter band and footer
- **Mobile:**
  - A solid bar with the logo at the start and search, bag and menu icons.
  - Search opens below the bar; the menu is a side drawer.
  - Photo-first hero; 2-column editorial categories and product cards.
  - Featured lot followed by a list; swipeable storefront cards;
    accordions.
  - No floating elements.

## Option 4 — Discovery Commerce (new; Auction Commerce removed)

- **Header:**
  - A compact bar: logo, a Discover button (visual category flyout), a wide
    search pill, the delivery city, watchlist, account and cart.
  - A scrolling row of shortcut chips below it: Deals, New in, Ending soon,
    Live now, Pallets, Under ⃁200, Grade A and new.
- **Hero:** a discovery board. A centre collection tile with a product
  collage, flanked by Deal of the day and New in on one side and Live now
  and Ending soon on the other.
- **Section order:**
  1. Discovery board
  2. Category explorer: large pastel category cards with product cut-outs,
     plus quick collection chips
  3. Price drops: a Buy Now deals rail
  4. Auctions within the marketplace: a Live now card beside an Ending soon
     rail, plus upcoming events
  5. Collections: 3 themed collections, each with a 2x2 product grid
  6. Shop by seller: seller collection cards in a rail
  7. New in: a feed of mixed-size cards (Buy Now, auctions, pallets)
  8. Why Khazna: trust cards, a how-it-works stepper, grade chips
  9. Footer with newsletter
- **Mobile:** a sticky search field with shortcut chips; the board as a
  2-column bento; everything else as swipe rails; "New in" as a 2-column
  feed; no bottom tab bar.
