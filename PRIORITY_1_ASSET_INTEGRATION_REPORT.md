| A7 | 992 × 1586 | 496 · 744 · 992 | 61 KB | quality 82 |

- 24 files, 2.0 MB in total. Against the masters, fidelity is 37.5–41.7 dB PSNR.
- The widest derivative of each asset is the master at its own size. Nothing was stretched or upscaled.
- Every image uses `srcset` with a `sizes` value that matches its rendered width. For example, the Option 2 hero canvas is 920–1728 CSS px wide, and at 1440 px the browser picks the 1920 w file.
- The three hero images load eagerly with high fetch priority. The recliner cut-out loads lazily, since it is below the fold.
- Option 4 serves A6 and A7 through one `<picture>`, so each device downloads only the composition it shows.
- Measured download per page (1× screens):
  - Option 4: A7 496 w at 320–390 px (744 w at 639 px), A6 960 w at 640–768 px, A6 1440 w at 1024–1440 px, A6 1920 w at 1920 px. Never both compositions.
  - Option 2 and Option 3: one hero file per page (A3 or A4; A5).

## 6. Responsive crop decisions

**Option 2 hero (A3 English / A4 Arabic).**
- **How the crop works.** The photograph sits on a 4:1 canvas that covers the hero strip the way `object-fit: cover` would. Its position is set by `--px` / `--py`, which work as object-position fractions. The price pin, its leader line and its dot are drawn inside that canvas, so the dot stays on the chair at every width and in both languages. Nothing is mirrored.
- **Pin geometry**, in master pixels:
  - English: card corner (1760, 60); dot on the recliner's right arm panel (2105, 445).
  - Arabic: card corner (730, 60); dot on the left arm panel (330, 455).

| Width | Layout | Crop |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 1728 px | Card over photo | Full image width, trimmed vertically (`--py` 0.25). At 1920 this keeps y ≈ 16–580 of 627. |
| 1440–1727 | Card over photo | Centred (`--px` 0.5). At 1440 this is the manifest's 3.33:1 crop, x 209–2299. |
| 1200–1439 | Card over photo | English biased right (0.75), Arabic left (0.25), so the chair clears the card. |
| 1024–1199 | Card over photo | English flush right (1.0), Arabic flush left (0). This is the manifest's tablet crop: A3 x 1003–2508, A4 x 0–1505. |
| < 1024 | Copy stacked above a photo strip, pin docked below it | English 0.94, Arabic 0.10. This matches the manifest's phone crops (A3 x 1360–2426, A4 x 150–1216) at 390 px and keeps the desk and chair in view from 320 to 1023 px. |

Why the copy stacks below 1024 px: from 768 to about 970 px the chair cannot be clear of a 470 px copy card. This follows the manifest ("put copy outside the strip when narrow; dock the price pin"). The stacking breakpoint moved from 768 to 1024 px, which makes the tablet hero taller (≈ 745 px at 768). This is the one layout change on Option 2, and it exists only to keep the photograph's chair visible.

**Option 3 furniture tile (A5).**
- **Crop.** `object-fit: cover` with focus 20 % 30 %. This keeps the whole chair in view across every tile ratio the mosaic produces, from 1.06:1 at 1440 down to 0.61:1 at 768 px in Arabic. At 1440 it matches the manifest's crop to within about 15 px, and the floor band stays clear.
- **Overlay placement.** Before this change, the Furniture label ran under the tote card at 1200–1300 px, where the tile is narrower than the 1440 composition. The label and tote card now sit side by side on the photograph's floor only when the tile is at least 512 px wide (a container query). On narrower tiles the label moves above the card, as it already did below 1200 px, and gets a soft local scrim because it then lies over the room rather than the floor. The tote card, its position and its content are unchanged.

**Option 4 hero (A6 wide / A7 phone).**

| Width | Photograph | Crop and placement |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 1364 px | A6 behind the centred copy | Full 433 px height; centred cover crop (3.33:1 at 1440 = manifest crop x 209–2299; vertical trim only above 1728 px) |
| 1200–1363 | A6 behind the centred copy | Bottom-aligned, height `31.75vw` (never cropped narrower than ~3.15:1), with a short fade into the wall colour above. The tote, chair, lamp and washer stay in view; the suitcase's right edge is trimmed at the narrow end. |
| 640–1199 | A6 as a strip under the copy | 10:3 (manifest crop), top faded into the wall colour (#dccbba sampled from A6). All products are shown, with nothing over them. |
| < 640 | A7 | Full width along the bottom of the hero, never cropped at the sides. The copy sits on the upper wall, and the bottom padding (`57vw`) keeps the buttons above the products. |

The search had to narrow on desktop. The manifest's clear content core (x 650–1700) is 725 px wide at 1440, and A6's leather chair starts at x 1736. The segmented search was 767 px wide at 1440, which would have covered the chair's arm. It now grows with the viewport up to 640 px (`min(640px, 48.7vw − 24px)`: 560 px at 1200, 640 px from about 1366 px) and stops about 12 px short of the chair. Everything else in the hero is unchanged: eyebrow, headline, subheading, search controls and behaviour, and buttons.
