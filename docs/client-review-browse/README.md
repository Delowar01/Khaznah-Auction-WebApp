# Browse redesign — screenshots (all four options)

Evidence for `CLIENT_REVIEW_BROWSE_REPORT.md` (repository root).

**How the images were made**

- Production build of the `design-preview/` app.
- Clock frozen at the same moment for every capture, so all countdowns
  match.
- All images loaded, animations off, presentation bar hidden.
- Every page is shown in its default state: all lots, ordered by Ending
  soonest.

**What you will see that is not part of the design**

- The small dark tab at the side of every page reopens the hidden
  presentation bar. It belongs to the preview, not to the design.
- On phones, Option 1's fixed bottom tab bar appears near the top of its
  full-page captures, because that is where it sits on the first screen.
- Option 1's desktop filter rail is sticky and scrolls on its own, so
  full-page captures show only its first screen of filters.

## Comparison sheet

`browse-four-options-comparison.jpg` places the four 1440 px English pages in
a 2 × 2 grid.

- Each tile is the top 2,000 px of the page: header, page head, live
  auction, sale-mode switch, filters and the first rows of lots.
- The full pages are the files above.
- The sheet shows that all four solve the same marketplace task, that each
  looks different, and that each belongs to its own homepage.
